6-Month Comprehensive Store Warranty

At A TO Z MOBILES, every certified pre-owned smartphone includes an official 6-Month Store Warranty covering both hardware components and manufacturing functional defects.

  • Display & Touchscreen: Covered for touch responsiveness failure and genuine display panel malfunction.
  • Motherboard & Chipset: Covered for spontaneous restarts, unexpected freezing, or charging circuit issues.
  • Camera Sensors & OIS: Covered for autofocus failure, lens blur, or sensor defects.
  • Battery Performance: Guaranteed to retain certified health capacity under normal usage during the warranty window.

Warranty Exclusions: Accidental physical drops, liquid spillage, third-party unauthorized disassembly or unauthorized firmware rooting void the warranty.

7-Day Hassle-Free Replacement Policy

We offer an unconditional 7-Day replacement window starting from the day of confirmed delivery. If you encounter any technical glitch or if the device does not match the inspected grade, we arrange a free doorstep reverse pickup and ship a replacement or issue a full refund.

  • Device must be returned with all original accessories provided (adapter, cable, warranty card, and box).
  • All personal cloud accounts (Apple iCloud, Google Account, Samsung Account) must be signed out and device reset.
  • Physical IMEI matching our database dispatch logs must be intact.
